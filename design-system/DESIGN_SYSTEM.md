# 🎨 Saikumar Digital Lab & Studio — Master Design System Specification (v3.0)

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision
Saikumar Digital Lab & Studio is an esteemed digital crafting and high-end gifting brand located in Miyapur, Hyderabad. The visual identity bridges **warm emotional gifting** (warm crimson red & saffron gold) with **elite corporate sophistication** (deep forest green & refined warm beige).

---

## 2. Color System & Semantic Tokens

### A. Customized Gifts & Home Highlighting
- **Primary Red:** `#FC3C1E` (Active, vibrant celebration)
- **Deep Red Accent:** `#C62828` / `#8E1B1B`
- **Secondary Gold / Yellow:** `#FD9B00` (Gifting warm glow)
- **Warm Cream Surface:** `#FFF8ED`
- **Noodle Cream:** `#FFEFCB`
- **Dark Neutral Text:** `#191919`
- **Secondary Text:** `#5F5F5F`

### B. Corporate Gifts & Executive Branding
- **Deep Forest Green:** `#12281C` (Header category accent & dark surfaces)
- **Primary Executive Green:** `#1F513D`
- **Refined Beige:** `#F1DDC0`
- **Warm Light Beige:** `#F5EBDD`
- **Muted Gold:** `#C8A96B`

---

## 3. Typography System
- **Primary Sans UI Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Manrope` (Clean, hyper-readable modern Indian ecommerce standard)
- **Display Serif Font:** `Playfair Display` (Applied purposefully to editorial headlines, gifting heroes, and signature banners)
- **Hierarchy:**
  - `Display XL`: 56px - 64px, ExtraBold 800
  - `H1`: 40px - 48px, Bold 700
  - `H2`: 32px - 36px, SemiBold 600
  - `H3`: 24px - 28px, SemiBold 600
  - `Body Large`: 18px, Regular 400
  - `Body`: 15px, Regular 400
  - `Button / Caption`: 13px - 14px, Bold 600, Tracking Wide

---

## 4. Spacing, Radii & Depth
- **Border Radius:**
  - Micro: `8px` (`rounded-lg`)
  - Cards: `16px` (`rounded-2xl`)
  - Modals / Hero Containers: `24px` (`rounded-3xl`)
  - Interactive Action Pills: `9999px` (`rounded-full`)
- **Visual Depth:** Restrained shadows (`0 10px 30px -4px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`), subtle 1px border contrast (`#E8DFD1`), zero arbitrary glassmorphism.

---

## 5. Components & UX Blueprint
1. **Header:** Clean ecommerce navigation with user's official logo, mega-menu on "Categories", direct category triggers, search popup, gift cart counter.
2. **Hero:** High-conversion editorial layout with photo elements, headline, and immediate customization action.
3. **Product Customization Modal (Image 4 Specification):**
   - Left: Live Preview (updates instantly when photo is uploaded)
   - Right: Drag & Drop upload (Single/Multiple files with file limits), Text engraving field, Occasion selector, Quantity picker, and Instant Order/Enquiry.
4. **Corporate Enquiry Modal:** Corporate branding options, logo vector upload, company details, bulk volume estimator.
5. **Real Checkout Process Flow:** Seamless transition from shortlisted items to customer details, delivery address, payment option review, and instant receipt confirmation.
