## 📐 Spacing & Radius Rules
- **Card Corners:** `rounded-2xl` (16px) or `rounded-3xl` (24px)
- **Buttons:** `rounded-full` (Pill format)
- **Section Margins:** 64px to 80px desktop, 40px to 48px mobile
- **Content Max-Width:** 1280px (`max-w-7xl mx-auto`)

---

## ✨ Motion & Interaction Engine
- **Anime.js:** Dropdown item staggering, section entrance reveals, smooth SVG path animations
- **Framer Motion / Motion.dev:** Route transitions, layout morphing, modal dialog popovers, cart drawer slides
- **Kokonut UI:** Slide-text buttons, glowing featured borders, shimmer badges, number tickers
- **Auto-rotating Banners:** 6.5s interval with pause-on-hover and reduced-motion fallback
